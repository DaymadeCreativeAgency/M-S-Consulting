### Not Your Average Internship

*"The experience I’ve gained at M&S is far and above more valuable than my experiences at other organizations. I’m given important tasks and am continually learning in ways that benefit me now and down the road. That, combined with a positive, hardworking culture of the best consultants in the field, results in one of the most fertile grounds for young professionals. If you want to do great work and be rewarded for it, come to M&S."*

Your college education is a good foundation to begin your real learning. We allow you to get your hands dirty from day one. You’ll enjoy an environment where **you’re challenged to find solutions and encouraged to ask questions.** Surrounding yourself with friendly and helpful professionals who are experts in their field will grow and motivate you. We want to pour our knowledge and understanding into you, but we expect you’ll pour your passion and excitement back into our organization and the projects you’re working on. **You’re treated as a valued asset, because you are.**
