    Survey Results for “COVID-19 Impacts on Higher Education”

  Our survey results compile faculty responses on diverse issues related to higher education experience in the COVID-19 pandemic. While speaking to the challenges and frustrations of the abrupt transition to online delivery in Spring 2020, respondents also had many ideas about how they and their institutions could strengthen their students’ educational outcomes.
Our analysis of this data considers the challenges and critiques offered by our respondents but focuses on the constructive suggestions participants offered on how to move forward in a situation that continues to present many challenges.

    Strategic Procure-to-Pay Assessment

  Shrinking budgets and increasing operating costs are placing higher education procurement departments under increased pressure to both engage campus users as organizational partners and serve the institution as purchasing knowledge experts. In addition, procurement departments continue to be responsible for ensuring appropriate controls and compliance as well as enabling increased buying power across campus.
