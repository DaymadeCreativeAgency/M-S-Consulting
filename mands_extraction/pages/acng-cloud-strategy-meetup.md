## **About ACNG**

The **Allegheny Cloud Network Group** was formed to provide a forum for professionals to exchange practical strategies, insights, and product knowledge related to cloud technologies. Our mission is to deliver content that directly benefits members in their work — whether through peer-led presentations, vendor demos, or hands-on discussions.

Membership is free and open to all professions. ACNG operates through the support of sponsors and volunteers. Events are designed to support local collaboration and avoid conflicts with other industry meetups.
