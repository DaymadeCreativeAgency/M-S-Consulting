# **The Appalachia Software Factory**

The Appalachia Software Factory (ASF) is M&S Consulting's homegrown initiative to develop custom software solutions while investing in the people and potential of West Virginia. ASF connects enterprise-grade software development with regional economic growth by growing talent, supporting startups, and delivering high-quality products from the heart of Appalachia.

## Built in West Virginia, Built to Scale

ASF began with a simple question: What if world-class software could be designed and developed in West Virginia? What if the future of tech included Appalachia, not just Silicon Valley?
Our answer is a distributed team of developers, data scientists, and product strategists based in Appalachia—building real solutions for enterprises, startups, and public-sector partners.

ASF is rooted in the belief that the region’s legacy of craftsmanship, resilience, and creativity is a natural fit for software development. With strong universities, growing infrastructure, and an emerging tech ecosystem, Appalachia has the capacity and culture to lead.

Most importantly, people here want to stay. ASF creates opportunities that allow talent to thrive locally—no relocation required.

## A Unique Model

ASF is more than a development shop. It's a platform for collaboration, innovation, and long-term growth.

Early-career developers contribute to real projects under experienced mentorship.

We build in small, outcome-focused sprints to reduce risk and maximize learning.

Developers gain experience on both client solutions and venture development.

Builders can earn equity in the ventures they help create.

ASF is a blueprint for what’s possible when vision and execution align in unexpected places. It demonstrates that innovation can come from anywhere—and thrive.
