# **CASE STUDY**

INTRODUCTION

Helping a high-impact public sector organization isn’t about ticking boxes or meeting KPIs. It’s about supporting people who are tasked with serving the public good—safeguarding lives, upholding justice, and operating at the intersection of security and service.

At M&S Consulting, we didn’t just show up to "do the job." We showed up to transform how the organization worked—because when the stakes are this high, “good enough” won’t cut it.

The Challenge

The real catalyst? A surprise multi-million-dollar licensing bill from a legacy platform. The agency felt blindsided. That frustration turned into action. Leadership made a bold decision: move off the proprietary system entirely and shift to a more flexible, open-source alternative.

But the challenge was bigger than software. They were also contending with the ongoing burden of managing large-scale physical infrastructure. Hardware planning and maintenance was monopolizing internal resources. The goal was clear: shift from capital expenses (CapEx) to operational expenses (OpEx), and redirect time and energy toward higher-impact outcomes for the communities they serve.

Work We Did

We helped modernize several interrelated systems critical to the organization’s operational ecosystem. These weren’t just migrations—they were strategic upgrades to support evolving business and compliance demands.

We guided the transition from restrictive legacy platforms to open-source infrastructure and laid the architectural groundwork for a cloud-first future. Our cloud architects helped define scalable patterns and security standards tailored to mission-critical needs.

This wasn’t a simple “lift-and-shift.” We helped reimagine operations—freeing up internal teams from managing monolithic data centers so they could focus on delivering faster, more secure, and more adaptable services.

At the same time, the organization embraced a cultural shift—adopting a SAFe framework and revamping how teams worked. We helped implement the right tooling and collaboration platforms to support this new agile mindset. Our support extended across platform integration, training, and long-term collaboration.

The results

Today, the organization operates with greater speed, security, and resilience. The impact is measurable not only in uptime and cost savings—but in operational freedom.

- Moved off a costly and rigid legacy platform, reducing long-term risk and expenses

- Enabled cloud-native operations with elastic scalability

- Strengthened core systems with automated security and compliance monitoring

- Rolled out agile practices and platforms across multiple internal teams

The impact

Three transformations were unfolding at once: exiting legacy platforms, shifting to cloud infrastructure, and adopting agile frameworks. The success wasn’t just about the tools. It came down to:

- Building trust through delivery and transparency

- Staying nimble while working across multiple efforts in parallel

- Elevating teams through tools, training, and confidence

Conclusion

This wasn’t a short-term project. It was a long-term shift in posture, culture, and capability. Today, this organization continues to evolve, and we’re proud to walk alongside them.
