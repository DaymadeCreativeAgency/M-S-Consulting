# **CASE STUDY**

INTRODUCTION

One mid-sized company found itself at that exact crossroads. After years of growth, their aging systems—once dependable—had become bottlenecks, slowing down operations and making it harder to scale effectively.

Realizing it was time to act, they chose to invest in a modernization effort. M&S Consulting was brought in to help guide the transition, not by replacing systems ourselves, but by leading the project with structure, clarity, and a focus on long-term success.

The Challenge

The organization had been using the same ERP system for years. At one time, it had fit their needs perfectly. But over time, the cracks began to show. Manual processes became burdensome. Data entry errors created costly delays. Month-end closings dragged on longer than necessary.

Sticking with the old system wasn’t just inefficient—it was holding them back from reaching their full potential.

Work We Did

Before any decisions could be made about new systems, we needed a deep understanding of how things were working—and where they weren’t. Through **Process Impact Blueprinting Workshops**, M&S partnered with key stakeholders and subject matter experts across the company to document operations, identify bottlenecks, and uncover hidden risks.

This step was about more than just mapping workflows. It was about building alignment, breaking down silos, and giving teams a shared understanding of the road ahead.

Armed with a clear blueprint, the company evaluated several ERP options. M&S played a critical role in the decision-making process—helping leadership weigh their choices, clarify priorities, and ensure the selected system would meet both current needs and future goals.

Ultimately, they chose a modern cloud-based ERP platform that offered stronger automation, better integration, and the flexibility needed to grow.

While we didn’t handle the technical implementation, M&S served as the **project management partner** throughout the transition, helping the team stay focused, organized, and on track.

Every successful transformation needs more than a great solution—it needs someone to guide the process.

By staying proactive and transparent, we helped minimize disruptions, resolve issues quickly, and keep the project moving forward.

M&S provided structure through an agile framework, offering:

- Clear milestones and deliverables
- Real-time tracking tools
- Weekly check-ins and risk management
- Ongoing communication with leadership and key stakeholders

The results

Even before the full rollout was complete, the organization began seeing real improvements:

- Manual tasks were reduced through automation

- Reporting processes became faster and more accurate

- Collaboration across departments improved noticeably

The project also had unexpected ripple effects. Teams that once worked in silos found new ways to collaborate. Business leaders gained new visibility into operations. The organization wasn’t just modernizing technology, they were building a stronger foundation for future growth.
