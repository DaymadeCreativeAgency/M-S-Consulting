### AI That Works the Way Teams Do

Most AI tools today are built for solo users. You open a chat, ask a question, get a response, and move on. But that’s not how most teams operate.

Braided flips the script by creating a shared space where business teams can:

- Collaborate in real time with AI

- Share prompts and outputs across departments

- Compare results from multiple language models side by side

Instead of scattered tools and inconsistent workflows, Braided brings clarity and cohesion to how teams explore, prompt, and problem-solve with AI.

### Built to Bridge Gaps in the AI Landscape

The Braided team came together with a clear mission: make enterprise-grade AI usable, shareable, and secure. Their backgrounds in machine learning, systems architecture, and business strategy shaped the product in powerful ways:

- **Jay Mason** saw a need for a secure way to bring multiple models into one tool without vendor lock-in.

- **John McCauley** designed Braided to stay ahead of the fast-changing AI ecosystem with plug-and-play flexibility.

- **Jared Icenhower** focused on making AI accessible to everyday business users—not just tech insiders.

With Braided, teams don’t have to choose just one AI model. They can:

- Switch between models like ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and more within the same conversation

- Tailor prompts to specific outcomes and compare how each model performs

- Use AI as a shared teammate in brainstorming, writing, analysis, and planning

### Addressing Real Business Concerns

Security and compliance are top of mind for IT leaders. Braided meets those concerns head-on:

- Enterprise-ready platform with vetted model integrations

- Prompts and responses are never used to train external models

- Controlled environment to prevent data leakage from copy/paste habits

The result? A safer, more centralized way to scale AI use across teams without compromising sensitive business data.

### Designed for the 90%, Not the Niche

Braided doesn’t aim to be everything for everyone. Instead, it delivers high-impact features for business users who want to:

- Draft reports, emails, or documentation faster

- Summarize research and meeting notes

- Solve problems collaboratively without losing context

Rather than chasing trends, the product is shaped by user needs. Biweekly updates ensure Braided evolves based on what matters most to its community.

### What’s Next: Agents and Customization

On the roadmap:

- **Agentic AI Support**: Custom agents that can perform tasks like generating Google Docs with pre-formatted sections or querying enterprise data.

- **Meta Instructions & Prompt Templates**: Project-level settings that save time and ensure consistency across every AI interaction.

- **A Braided-Owned AI Model**: A secure, controllable alternative for sensitive use cases that require more tailored outputs and data handling.

These features all push Braided closer to becoming the intelligent backbone of modern business collaboration.
