### A New Kind of Software Model

The Appalachian Software Factory (ASF) isn’t your typical dev shop. Built by a team of makers, tinkerers, and problem-solvers, ASF offers custom software solutions for enterprise clients, government partners, and growing startups, all from the mountains of West Virginia.

What makes ASF different is the dual-focus model:

- **Client work:** Delivering robust custom software, dashboards, tooling, and analytics solutions to organizations of all sizes.

- **Startup incubation:** Giving team members the opportunity to launch their own ideas, with equity, mentorship, and real-world experience built in.

This blend of service and product creation fuels deep skill development and builds talent that’s versatile, entrepreneurial, and impact-driven.

### Why Appalachia?

There’s a deep craftsman spirit in Appalachia. ASF taps into that legacy by creating space for coders, designers, and builders who want to stay rooted in the region while working on cutting-edge technology.

- Strong university pipeline (especially in Morgantown)

- High quality of life and natural beauty

- Historically overlooked but rich in problem-solving and craftsmanship

The result is a growing ecosystem of homegrown talent, people who want to live where they love and build things that matter.

### A Flexible Approach to Software Delivery

ASF doesn’t believe in one-size-fits-all. Depending on the client, the team might:

- Build new applications from scratch (greenfield development)

- Join enterprise teams to maintain and expand legacy systems

- Integrate with existing product teams for feature enhancements or problem-solving

Rather than giant, months-long builds, ASF emphasizes an iterative, MVP-first approach. Every project starts with a deep understanding of the user, their needs, and the business value of the solution. Then comes rapid development, hands-on feedback, and continuous improvement.

### Human Impact Through Code

For ASF, success isn’t about lines of code, it’s about leverage. If a week of dev work can save thousands of hours across a client’s workforce, that’s a win. If a new tool helps reduce error rates, improve decision-making, or streamline an essential process, the impact is real.

Custom software isn’t just a tech solution. It’s a tool that solves human problems, often at scale.

### Innovation from the Inside Out

ASF has already launched and supported several impactful ventures, including:

- **Momentum:** A greenhouse operations platform that tracks labor, logistics, and timing in plant production. It’s helping growers manage complexity and scale with precision.

- **Upcoming startup (in stealth):** A productivity tool built around smart clipboard functionality, leveraging contextual AI to automate common actions and accelerate workflows.

These ventures aren’t side projects. They’re built and supported by the same talent delivering top-tier solutions for clients, proof that ASF’s internal model cultivates innovation.

### Looking Ahead

The vision for ASF is clear: more builders, more ventures, more impact. The team is expanding, the pipeline is growing, and the possibilities are wide open.

ASF is proving that world-class software can come from anywhere, and that Appalachia is a place where technology and craftsmanship meet.
