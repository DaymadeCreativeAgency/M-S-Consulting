M&S Consulting, a leading provider of innovative digital transformation solutions, is proud to announce its nomination for the [2025 Elev8 GovCon Award](https://orangeslices.ai/its-time-to-elev8-govcon-2025-edition-recognizing-those-government-contracting-companies-who-are-doing-it-the-right-way/). This prestigious award recognizes companies in the federal government contracting sector that demonstrate excellence across eight key areas, setting higher standards for culture, engagement, and innovation.

The Elev8 award evaluates companies based on outstanding characteristics in the following areas: Good Partners, Growing Talent, Exemplifying Innovation, Philanthropy, Industry Engagement, Inclusivity and Diversity, Sustainability Practices, and Investment for the Sake of Betterment. M&S Consulting has been recognized for its commitment to these principles, which are essential to success in today’s competitive federal marketplace.

**"We are honored to be recognized as a nominee for the Elev8 GovCon Award,"** said Tina Mascaro, Associate Partner at M&S Consulting. **"This nomination reflects our ongoing dedication to our clients, our people, and the communities we serve. It highlights the culture of collaboration and innovation we foster at M&S, which allows us to exceed expectations in every aspect of our work."**

As a company that takes pride in building long-lasting partnerships, M&S Consulting consistently goes above and beyond for its clients by offering tailored solutions that address unique challenges. This nomination underscores M&S’s role as a trusted advisor, consistently bringing innovation and forward-thinking strategies to help government agencies navigate complex technological advancements.

M&S Consulting also takes great pride in fostering an inclusive and diverse workplace. As a women-owned, small disadvantaged business, the company promotes a culture of open communication, mentorship, and continuous learning, ensuring that all voices are heard and that employees have the support they need to excel.

Beyond its commitment to talent development, M&S Consulting is deeply rooted in giving back to the community. From supporting local charities to offering mentorship programs at institutions like West Virginia University and MIT, M&S demonstrates its commitment to philanthropy both locally and globally.

**"Our nomination for the Elev8 award reaffirms our dedication to not just our clients but to our broader mission of driving innovation, supporting our community, and creating a workplace that fosters growth and opportunity for all,"** added Daidre Fanis, Associate Partner at M&S Consulting.

As the company continues to grow and innovate, M&S Consulting remains committed to its core values of integrity, collaboration, and excellence, ensuring that it continues to be a beacon for talent, partners, and clients in the federal marketplace.

The Elev8 GovCon Award winners will be announced in early 2025, and M&S Consulting is honored to stand among the nominees who exemplify what it means to "do it right."
