### Solving a Universal Problem: Getting Lost

Everyone has experienced the frustration of showing up somewhere new and having no idea where to go. Whether it’s a college campus, hospital, corporate complex, or event venue, the stress of poor wayfinding can negatively impact how people feel about your organization.

get2there aims to solve that. It’s a digital map platform designed to help people navigate unfamiliar environments with ease—without needing to download an app or decode a static PDF.

### Designed for Visitors, Managed with Ease

Unlike traditional maps or kiosks, get2there provides:

- QR-code access, no downloads required

- Searchable maps tailored to each location

- Real-time updates and customization for events or temporary changes

From finding a meeting room to navigating a multi-use sports complex, the experience is intuitive and user-first. Visitors can search by name, category, or keyword—whether they’re looking for a restroom or a retail store.

And for the facilities team? The lift is light. Organizations simply provide existing map files and information, and the get2there team builds and manages the experience. Updates are handled collaboratively, ensuring maps stay accurate without requiring in-house development resources.

### More Than Maps: Supporting Better Experiences

get2there isn't just about directions. It’s about:

- Reducing visitor stress and confusion

- Improving staff efficiency by lowering the need for manual wayfinding help

- Enhancing overall guest experience, especially in high-pressure environments like hospitals or arenas

Organizations can also use get2there to:

- Track usage trends through analytics

- Provide accessibility information

- Monetize event-specific maps or sponsorships

### Where It Fits Best

While any space with foot traffic can benefit from get2there, early traction is especially strong in:

- Healthcare and hospitals

- College and university campuses

- Large corporate offices and training centers

- Convention centers and resort properties

- Mixed-use complexes (retail, sports, government, and residential)

It’s particularly useful for organizations that want a better guest experience but lack the internal tech team to build their own tools.

### Looking Ahead

CEO Taryn Quinlan brings 25+ years of go-to-market experience and customer empathy to the table. Under her leadership, get2there is building for the future with:

- Smarter integrations (like traffic flow data and location-based services)

- Visitor analytics and insights to support better decision-making

- Tools to unlock new revenue streams through map sponsorship or premium experiences

The goal isn’t just to make better maps. It’s to help organizations meet modern expectations and deliver a smoother, more thoughtful experience to every guest.
