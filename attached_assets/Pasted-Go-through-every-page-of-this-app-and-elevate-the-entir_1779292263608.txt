Go through every page of this app and elevate the entire UI/UX experience to a high-craft, visually distinctive standard. I want each page to feel unique and intentional, not like the same template with different content swapped in.For each page, do the following:Visual identity per page. Give each page its own personality through layout structure, typographic hierarchy, color accenting, and motion. Pages should share a coherent design system (consistent type scale, spacing rhythm, component library, color tokens) but feel distinct in how they use it. A landing page should feel different from a dashboard, which should feel different from a settings page, which should feel different from a detail view.Layout sophistication. Move beyond stacked cards and centered columns. Use asymmetric grids, considered whitespace, intentional density variation, anchored visual elements, and layered compositions where appropriate. Every page should have a clear focal point and a deliberate reading path.Typography as a design element. Use a real type scale with meaningful contrast between display, headline, body, and caption sizes. Vary weight, tracking, and treatment to create hierarchy. Consider where oversized type, mixed weights, or editorial-style headlines could elevate a page.Motion and microinteractions. Add purposeful animation: page transitions, scroll-triggered reveals, hover states with personality, loading states that feel designed rather than default, subtle parallax or depth where it serves the content. Nothing gratuitous, but nothing flat either.Detail work. Custom iconography or a curated icon set used consistently. Real imagery or illustration where placeholder content currently lives. Proper empty states, error states, and loading states on every page. Thoughtful copy in buttons, headings, and helper text.Polish pass. Audit every page for visual bugs, spacing inconsistencies, alignment issues, color contrast problems, and responsive breakpoints. Test on mobile, tablet, and desktop. Fix anything that looks unfinished.Before you start, do a walkthrough of every page in the app and propose what each one's distinct visual identity should be, then wait for my approval before implementing. I want to see your design thinking before you touch the code.
